How the Press-Open Mechanism Works</h2><p>A disc top cap — also called a press-top cap, push-pull cap, or snap-open dispensing cap — is a one-piece or two-piece PP closure with a hinged disc that opens a central orifice when pressed and reseals when pressed again. The mechanism works by deflecting the disc plate against a spring-loaded collar ring: downward pressure on the outer ring pushes the inner plug away from the orifice seat, allowing product to flow; releasing pressure allows the spring tension in the PP hinge to pull the plug back into the sealed position.</p><p>The key engineering parameters that determine disc top cap performance are:</p><table class="spec-table"><thead><tr><th>Parameter</th><th>Technical Detail</th><th>Consumer Impact</th><th>SUNSHINE Standard</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Actuation force (open)</td><td>Force required to press disc open, typically 3–8 N</td><td>Too stiff → accessibility barrier for elderly/kids; Hinge Mechanism and Design Variants</h2><p>A flip top cap — also called a snap-top, hinged cap, or flip cap — uses a living hinge to connect a hinged lid to a dispensing base. Unlike a disc top cap where the disc is pressed downward, a flip top lid is pulled or levered upward from a snapped-closed position. The living hinge is an integral PP strip (typically 0.3–0.5 mm thick) that flexes without fatigue — a well-designed PP living hinge can withstand 1 million+ open/close cycles.</p><p>Flip top caps come in three primary design variants:</p><table class="spec-table"><thead><tr><th>Variant</th><th>Hinge Position</th><th>Lid Opening Angle</th><th>Typical Application</th><th>Orifice Control</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Standard Flip Top</strong></td><td>Rear (180° hinge)</td><td>Opens 160–180°</td><td>Shampoo, conditioner, body wash (high volume)</td><td>Fixed orifice, 4–10 mm diameter</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Narrow Flip Top</strong></td><td>Rear (asymmetric hinge)</td><td>Opens 120–140°</td><td>Toothpaste, sunscreen, thick creams</td><td>Controlled orifice, 3–5 mm</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Side-Access Flip Top</strong></td><td>Side (90° hinge)</td><td>Opens 90–110°</td><td>Hand sanitizer, sports bottles, travel packs</td><td>Wide orifice, 8–15 mm</td></tr></tbody></table><p>The living hinge in PP is the most critical manufacturing dimension for flip top caps. If the glass neck has a 24/415 or 28/415 finish (common in prestige and pharmaceutical glass), you need a 415-thread disc cap — which requires custom tooling. Always confirm the GPI thread designation from your glass supplier before ordering cap samples.</p><p><strong>What’s the difference between a disc top cap and a push-pull cap?</strong></p><p>They are the same mechanism, different trade names. “Push-pull” is common in North America; “disc top” is more common in Europe and Asia. Both describe a closure where a central disc is pressed to open and pressed again to close. “Snap-top” typically refers to a flip top cap — a different mechanism with a hinged lid.</p><p><strong>Will a 28/410 disc top cap fit all 28mm bottles?</strong></p><p>No. 28mm refers to neck outer diameter, but the thread profile (410, 412, 415, 400) determines whether the cap engages correctly. SUNSHINE’s 28/410 disc caps (WP-1372, WP-1376) fit 28/410 bottle necks only. A 28/415 glass bottle neck or a 28/400 cleaning bottle neck requires a different cap. Test before ordering production quantities.</p><p><strong>How do I prevent my disc top cap from leaking in shipping?</strong></p><p>Three steps: (1) Specify Non-Spill standard on your purchase order — SUNSHINE tests this per batch; (2) Fill to maximum 85% bottle capacity to allow headspace compression during altitude changes in airfreight; (3) For international express courier shipments, add a tamper-seal band over the cap as secondary protection. Over 95% of disc top cap shipping leaks trace back to overfill, not cap failure.</p><p><strong>Can I source disc top caps and lotion pumps from SUNSHINE with matching finishes?</strong></p><p>Yes — this is a common B2B request.
