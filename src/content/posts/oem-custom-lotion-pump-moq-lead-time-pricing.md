The number is determined by three hard constraints:</p><h3>1. Injection Mold Cavities</h3><p>A standard lotion pump mold has 8–24 cavities. Each injection cycle produces 8–24 parts simultaneously, and a production run needs about 2,000–5,000 cycles to stabilize (mold temperature, material flow, cooling time). At 8 cavities × 3,000 cycles = 24,000 parts minimum before the process is stable. Running fewer cycles means higher scrap rate and inconsistent part quality — the factory loses money.</p><h3>2. Component Count</h3><p>A lotion pump has 8–12 individual components (body, piston, spring, ball valve, dip tube, actuator, closure, gasket, etc.). Each component comes from a different mold. The MOQ applies to the <em>assembly run</em>, not individual parts. A 28/410 pump will not fit a 24/410 bottle. Always confirm your bottle’s neck finish before ordering pumps. If you’re sourcing both bottle and pump from Sunshine Plastic, this is handled automatically — but if you’re using an existing bottle, measure twice.</p><h3>Pitfall 2: Ignoring Formula Compatibility</h3><p>Not all lotion pump gasket materials work with all formulations. Standard LDPE gaskets work for water-based lotions. But products containing alcohol, essential oils, alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs), or silicones may cause gasket swelling, leading to leakage or pump seizure. Always provide your formulation to the supplier for compatibility testing before committing to a production order.</p><h3>Pitfall 3: Assuming “Standard” Means the Same Thing Everywhere</h3><p>One factory’s “standard output 1.0ml” may be another’s “standard output 1.5ml.” One factory’s “MOQ 10,000” might include custom color; another might charge extra. Always get every spec in writing: output volume, actuation force (Newtons), closure material, gasket material, dip tube inner/outer diameter, carton quantity. A one-page spec sheet prevents a container of wrong pumps.</p><h2>Conclusion: Your OEM Lotion Pump Cheat Sheet</h2><table><tr><th>You Want</th><th>You’ll Likely Get</th><th>How to Improve It</th></tr><tr><td>Lower MOQ</td><td>10,000 standard / 20,000 lock pump</td><td>Start with standard color + closure; negotiate trial order terms</td></tr><tr><td>Faster lead time</td><td>15–30 days production</td><td>Order standard models in Q1–Q2 (off-peak); avoid custom colors if urgent</td></tr><tr><td>Better pricing</td><td>Volume-scaled; 15–20% lower at 100K+</td><td>Annual contract with quarterly shipments; combine multiple SKUs into one PO</td></tr><tr><td>Custom look without custom mold</td><td>6 closure types + custom color + silk screen</td><td>Layer UV coating + custom color + logo print — looks custom without mold cost</td></tr><tr><td>Quality assurance</td><td>6-stage QC with AQL sampling</td><td>Request QC report with every shipment; specify third-party inspection if needed</td></tr></table><hr><div style="margin:40px 0 32px;padding:24px 28px;background:#eef4fb;border-left:4px solid #2c5f8a;border-radius:6px;"><p style="margin:0 0 8px;font-size:.85rem;font-weight:700;letter-spacing:.06em;text-transform:uppercase;color:#2c5f8a;">Manufacturer Resource</p><p style="margin:0 0 12px;font-size:1.05rem;font-weight:600;color:#1a1a2e;">Ready to Source Lotion Pumps Direct from the Factory?</p><p style="margin:0 0 16px;font-size:.95rem;color:#444;">SUNSHINE Plastic (Ningbo Shaoshuai Plastic Industry Co., Ltd.) offers 68 SKUs, 10,000 pcs MOQ, 30-day lead time, and full OEM/ODM — ISO 9001 certified, factory-direct pricing.</p><p><a href="/lotion-pump-manufacturer/" style="display:inline-block;padding:10px 22px;background:#2c5f8a;color:#fff;text-decoration:none;border-radius:4px;font-size:.95rem;font-weight:600;">View Our Lotion Pump Range →</a></div><p><strong>About the Manufacturer:</strong> Ningbo Shaoshuai Plastic Industry Co., Ltd.
