FAQ

Questions Every Buyer Asks

What is your MOQ for lotion pumps?
Standard lotion pumps (24/410 and 28/410) start at 10,000 pcs per color/style. Fine mist sprayers with metal components start at 20,000 pcs. Custom molds: typically 50,000 pcs minimum.
How do I get a free sample?
Submit your inquiry via the form, specifying pump type and neck size. We prepare a physical sample within 7 days and ship it — you only pay courier costs (~$15–25 via DHL). No commitment required.
What is the lead time for custom color orders?
Custom color production takes 20 days after sample approval. New mold required? Add 15–20 days. We provide milestone updates via WhatsApp throughout.
Do you accept OEM branding (logo on pump)?
Yes. We support screen printing, UV printing, hot stamping, and water transfer printing. Provide artwork as AI or PDF vector file. Minimum branding run matches MOQ (10,000 pcs).
Which neck sizes are compatible with standard bottles?
We manufacture 24/410, 28/410, and 28/412 neck finishes — the three most common globally. Non-standard bottle? Send us a sample and we’ll confirm compatibility.
What quality testing do you perform?
Every batch undergoes pressure leak testing and pump cycle durability testing. We provide a QC report with photos. Third-party SGS inspection available on request.
